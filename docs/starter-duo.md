# Starter Duo — concept approval 01
Status: visual concept approved by project owner; production sprites and balance pending.
Two starting companions, choose one. Both families have three forms. The unchosen family should be obtainable later (design proposal).
 
| Family | Forms | Aspect | Combat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Moffi | Moffi → Mofflu → Moffaire | Gale / Lumen | Ranged magical DPS; mobility, distance control |
| Peepi | Peepi → Brooster → Galladius | Ember / Stone | Melee physical DPS; approach, sustained strikes, durability |

Visual reference: approved Starter Duo concept sheet 01 in design conversation. Moffi is petite, fluffy, cream-white with pale blue accents, expressive round eyes, a blue neckerchief with Aevoria emblem. Mofflu has long airy ears and floating ribbons; Moffaire retains cute compact face with wing-like ears and a golden floating halo. Avoid bulky/muscular Moffi. Peepi is a round cream chick, red comb, orange feet and red neckerchief; Brooster is a comedic muscular rooster; Galladius is a powerful martial rooster with red cape, gold accents and martial bracers. Distinct original designs; use reference only, not final animation sheets.

Candidate skill vocabulary: Moffi family Breeze Bolt, Light Flare, Sky Currents; Peepi family Peck Rush, Wing Slam, Fighting Spirit. No final coefficients, cooldowns, evolution requirements or stat values. Physical/magical damage and melee/ranged distance are independent from Aspect. One active Kin, manual skills; both starters viable at late game with investment. Preserve the Every Kin Matters principle and optional non-form-changing development proposal.

Production gate: original clean turnaround and pixel sprites, 8-direction movement/idle, attack and skill states, hurt, KO and bonding; check mobile scale, silhouette and animation continuity. Concept sheet is not a sprite atlas. No binary artwork was committed in this checkpoint.
