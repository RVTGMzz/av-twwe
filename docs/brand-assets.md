# Brand Assets — concept brief 02
Status: candidate visual direction, pending approval.
- AEVORIA / A WORLD WITHOUT END: original angular A mark, gold and turquoise, horizontal and stacked logo and standalone app mark.
- Aevin is a capture artifact family, never currency. Angular A-shaped open frame, suspended faceted core, simple silhouette, readable at 16–32px. Core and frame details identify grades, not color alone. Proposed grades: Aevin, Prime Aevin, Royal Aevin, Astral Aevin, Eternal Aevin. Special variants later. No spherical throwing mechanic. Use only on unconscious eligible Kin, resonance from the player's position, success/failure.
- Kinwell: universal healing/roster/storage service, turquoise-blue sign, distinct healing emblem (paired leaves and luminous central diamond); candidate function fast travel. Maintain recognizable sign across regional architecture.
- Aevin Mart: universal supplies shop, warm amber/copper sign, stylized Aevin crystal and open satchel emblem. Sell capture artifacts, restorative items and local goods. Not a healing center.
- Currency name separate and undecided; avoid coin imagery for capture items.
- Palette: Aether Mint #82D9D2, Ancient Gold #D9C38D, Deep Azure #254C59, Midnight #10202B; amber shop accent. Architecture uses detailed crisp top-down 2D pixel art, clear silhouettes, ancient-fantasy technology. Concept board is not production sprites or finalized trademark.
