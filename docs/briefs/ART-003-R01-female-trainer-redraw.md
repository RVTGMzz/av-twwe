# ART-003-R01 — Female Trainer sprite redraw (brief for a pixel artist or PixelLab)

> **Tóm tắt (cho chủ dự án):** Brief này giao cho họa sĩ pixel art, hoặc dùng làm hướng dẫn khi tạo bằng PixelLab, để vẽ lại Trainer nữ: 8 hướng đứng yên, sau đó walk 6 khung × 8 hướng. Mọi thông số kỹ thuật đều khớp pipeline hiện có, nên hình nộp về có thể nhập, kiểm tra và tách lớp ngay. Ảnh tham chiếu: `docs/briefs/female_redraw_reference.png`. Không gửi kèm sprite cũ bị loại.

Project: **Aevoria — A World Without End**, an original offline monster-taming RPG (mobile first, PC). Top-down, slightly tilted camera; premium 2D pixel art. All work must be original: no tracing or reuse of any commercial game's assets.

## 1. What to draw
**Phase A — Direction master (do this first, it gets owner approval before Phase B)**
- One standing pose of the female trainer in 8 directions: S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE.

**Phase B — Walk cycle (after Phase A is approved)**
- 6 frames per direction × 8 directions = 48 frames.
- Frame 0 must be the approved Phase A sprite, unchanged.

Idle (breathing) is generated by our pipeline from Phase A, so do not draw it.

## 2. Character design (authority: reference image, section B)
- Young female explorer. Cute but not childish; about **3.25 heads tall**. Natural shoulders and hips; arms reach about mid-thigh; legs readable, not stick-thin.
- **Silver/white hair** tied in a **side ponytail with a blue ribbon**, worn on **HER RIGHT side**.
- **Aviator goggles** on her head (brown leather strap, light-blue lenses), **blue eyes**, **blue scarf**.
- **Cream jacket** with blue panels and trim, gold buckles.
- Brown leather **belt with pouches** and brown **gloves**.
- **Short blue skirt over cream shorts**. The skirt hem and the shorts must read clearly as separate shapes.
- **Dark socks** and brown laced **boots**.
- The **hero pose** in reference B is the authority for details. The small 8-direction strip on the board is a guide for the turnaround only; it has a known error (see §4).
- She is her own design, not the male with different hair.

## 3. Technical specification (mandatory)
| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Canvas | **64 × 96 px** per sprite, one character per file |
| Scale | Same height and pixel density as the approved male (reference A): figure about 90 px tall, top of head at y≈4, feet on the ground line |
| Pivot / feet | Planted boots touch **y = 93** (2 px margin at the bottom), body horizontally centred |
| Background | Fully **transparent**. No background colour anywhere, **including the space between the legs** |
| Alpha | Hard alpha only (0 or 255). No soft edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur |
| Colours | At most **48 colours per sprite**, shared palette across all directions and frames |
| Style | Selective outlines (coloured/lighter outline on lit edges, not black everywhere), clean pixel clusters, top-left key light |
| Format | PNG (RGBA). We convert to lossless WebP ourselves |
| Mirroring | **Do not mirror** W↔E, SW↔SE or NW↔NE. The ponytail, ribbon and pouches are asymmetric, so every direction is drawn on its own |

## 4. Direction rules
- Direction order and names: **S, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E, SE** (S faces the camera).
- The **ponytail and ribbon stay on her right side in every direction**: screen-left when she faces S, screen-right when she faces N; on SW, SE, NW and NE follow the same body side. The board strip gets SW, NE, N and S wrong; follow the hero pose.
- Head, face, torso, arms and legs always face the same direction. Nothing rotates on its own.
- Side and diagonal views show depth: the near arm and leg in front, the far ones partly hidden.

## 5. Walk rules (Phase B)
6 frames: f0 standing (= Phase A), f1 contact (near leg forward), f2 passing, f3 transition, f4 contact (far leg forward), f5 passing. Timing in game: 130 / 100 / 110 / 130 / 100 / 110 ms.
- Boots always point in the **walking direction**. On contact the front foot is heel down; the back foot is heel up, pushing off from the toe. If the back foot is drawn as a heel strike, the walk reads as walking backwards.
- Near and far legs must clearly alternate between f1 and f4. The planted foot slides backward under the body over the cycle.
- Body rises 1 px on the passing frames. Arms swing opposite to the legs, with the hands moving 2–4 px. Hair, ponytail and skirt may sway 1–2 px but stay attached and on the same side.
- Head, face and outfit details keep the Phase A pixels; only the moving parts change.

## 6. Layers (strongly preferred)
Our game builds the character from separate layers, so outfits can change. Best delivery: for each sprite or frame, **one PNG per layer** on the same 64×96 canvas. The layers stacked in this order must reproduce the full sprite exactly:
`base_body` (skin + a plain neutral grey undergarment), `face`, `bottom` (skirt + shorts + socks), `shoes`, `top` (jacket incl. sleeves), `gloves`, `accessories` (belt, pouches, straps), `scarf`, `hair` (incl. ponytail), `hair_ribbon`, `headwear` (goggles).
- Draw each layer as **complete** as reasonable under the layers above it: body under the clothes, hair under the goggles and ribbon, jacket under the scarf and belt. This lets players remove items.
- If layered delivery is not possible, a flat sprite is acceptable. We then label and split it ourselves, but hidden areas will be missing.
- With PixelLab: generate the character from reference B, use its rotation/animation features for the 8 directions and the walk, then clean up by hand to the rules above.

## 7. File naming and delivery
- Phase A: `trainer_f_{dir}_64x96.png`, e.g. `trainer_f_sw_64x96.png`; with layers, `trainer_f_{dir}_{layer}.png`.
- Phase B: `walk_f_{dir}_f{0..5}.png`; with layers, `walk_f_{dir}_f{n}_{layer}.png`.
- `{dir}` in lowercase: s, sw, w, nw, n, ne, e, se.
- Also deliver one sheet image (8 directions, and one walk row per direction) and an animated GIF per direction for review.

## 8. Acceptance checks (we run these on delivery)
- `python tools/asset-pipeline/check_master.py` — canvas, transparency, hard alpha, ≤48 colours, no stray pixels, **no light halo/white specks on the edge**.
- `python tools/asset-pipeline/check_gaps.py` — no light background left between the legs or boots.
- `python tools/asset-pipeline/check_flicker.py` — walk frames do not add blinking bright pixels.
- Layers: stacking them must equal the full sprite pixel for pixel.
- Visual review on a **dark background** at 1× and 4× in the offline viewer `tools/asset-pipeline/viewer_v3/`.
- Final say: the project owner approves Phase A before Phase B starts.

## 9. Problems seen in earlier attempts (please avoid)
- Over-chibi proportions, then stick-thin and stiff ones.
- Ponytail flipping sides between directions; two ponytails or two hair masses overlapping.
- White specks along the outline; light background left between the legs.
- A walk that reads as walking backwards; frames that blink.
- Skirt and shorts merging into one pale block between the legs (current female sprite).

## 11. PixelLab workflow (owner choice, 2026-11-26)
- Connection: PixelLab MCP for Claude Code. The owner adds it with their own API token in their own terminal (the token is never pasted in chat or stored in the repo):
  `claude mcp add pixellab https://api.pixellab.ai/mcp -t http -H "Authorization: Bearer <YOUR_PIXELLAB_TOKEN>"`
  Docs: https://www.pixellab.ai/mcp. An 8-direction "Pro" character needs a Tier 1 subscription; check the current price at https://www.pixellab.ai.
- Generation: create the character from reference B (description + the hero image), 8 directions, transparent background. Then animate "walk" per direction. PixelLab sizes are mostly square (48/64/85/113/128/168…); pick the canvas where the figure is about 90 px tall, or export at 2x and import with `--scale 2`.
- Import: `python tools/asset-pipeline/import_sprites.py <pixellab_export_dir> assets/concepts/trainer-gate02-r01/female [--scale N]` places each direction on the 64x96 canvas (feet on y=93, centred, same offset for all frames of a direction), then run the §8 checks.
- PixelLab output is still a draft. Fix the ponytail side, the skirt/shorts read and the outline style by hand (Aseprite + PixelLab extension) before owner review.
