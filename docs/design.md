# AV-TWWE — Design baseline 0.1
Date: 2026-11-25. Status: pre-production. Vietnamese design notes; English proper names and terminology. Confirmed directions are separated from working proposals.

## Confirmed product pillars
- Aevoria — A World Without End; creatures are called Kin. Original IP, not a reskin or port.
- Fully offline, mobile landscape first; PC support desired. No required login, server, daily online check or monetization dependency.
- Real-time exploration and creature combat, with the player directing a single active Kin. Avoid a second player combat skill bar. Manual combat first; no auto farming, automatic target switching or automatic skill casts. Routine movement and attack commands may be assisted.
- Polished, expressive, top-down pixel art; eight-direction movement is a target. Crisp nearest-neighbor rendering, clear silhouettes, layered environments, readable effects; modern touch UI.
- Vast, awe-inspiring fantasy ecosystems, ancient technology and mysteries, varied civilizations, oversized creatures and scenic landscapes. Original geography and designs.
- Continuous world clock, manual time adjustment, NPC schedules, day/night encounters. No mandatory sleep, exhaustion penalty or daily crafting schedule. Crafting not in initial scope.
- Social relationships and dating of eligible adult NPCs; marriage and adventurer partners are later design candidates. No daily login or compulsory chores.
- Story grows in episodic Chronicles with new regions and mysteries. No forced final end to gameplay. Future Vietnam-inspired region/DLC, not the initial cultural setting.
- Capture requires a defeated/unconscious eligible Kin. No throwable spherical capture object. Every Kin can remain viable late game through effort; rare creatures may be stronger at acquisition.

## World/lore proposals
Aevra is an energy present in living and non-living matter. Awakening can create life when matter resonates with memory, environment or emotion. Normally rare awakenings become increasingly frequent due to a mysterious surge/leak. Aevin resonates with a Kin's life imprint; a defeated Kin becomes eligible for a bond. This is a working explanation, not final canon.
Kin origins (not combat elements): Wildborn (natural), Objectborn (objects), Taleborn (folklore), Dreamborn (emotion/dream), Relicborn (ancient machinery), Primordial (ancient life). Use original whimsical wordplay, expressive personalities, fairy-tale parodies and majestic or unsettling creatures. E.g. Eggscalibur (egg knight), Goldiladle (porridge ladle), Cindersole (glass slipper), Broomble (living broom); all placeholder names/designs. Do not copy specific third-party creature silhouettes or assets.
Opening candidate: a structure falls from above the cloud layers near a frontier settlement; a mysterious Kin and relic are discovered. The relic knows the protagonist's name and counts down to an unknown event. The energy surge initially creates comic local incidents, gradually revealing a deeper mystery. Do not resolve the world's ultimate mystery in release one.

## World activity candidates
Day/night and weather affect spawn tables and NPC routines; doc baseline proposes 48 real minutes per in-game day, editable after testing. Pause clock when game is closed. Ecological behaviors include peaceful, aggressive, herd, shy, territorial, migrating and apex. Unique named encounters, rare cosmetic variants, repeatable guild missions, boss hunts with simulated allies, exploration puzzles, sanctuary and optional housing/life skills. These are candidates, not all MVP commitments.

## Creative policy
English creature, item and skill names with Vietnamese dialogue/localization; no requirement for Vietnamese-themed naming. Original art, lore, sound and code; record provenance and licensing. No copied game content. Public repo; only public-safe design material.
