## Character brief
Both: original Aevoria fantasy explorers, young adult/teen hero, bright and charming but not childish; ~3–3.5 heads tall; arms and legs long enough to read clearly, never stick-thin; real torso volume with natural shoulders and hips; aviator goggles worn on the head.
- **Male:** naturally messy brown hair; aviator goggles on head; red scarf; fantasy explorer outfit in cream, brown and blue; bright, youthful face with personality.
- **Female:** silver/white hair tied in an off-centre (side) tie with a blue ribbon; aviator goggles on head; blue eyes; fantasy outfit in cream, blue and gold; her own silhouette and costume — not the male design with different hair.
- Option spread: vary costume cut/accessory and silhouette between options A/B/C while keeping the brief; keep the same proportions per gender across options.

## Technical standard
- Canvas: test one of 48x64, 64x80, 64x96 (state which; 64x80 recommended to start). Feet pivot at bottom centre, ~2 px margin. Transparent background.
- Premium 2D pixel art: selective outlining (coloured/lighter outline on lit edges, not black everywhere), deliberate pixel clusters, harmonious palette (≤ 48 colours per sprite), lighting with depth (top-left key light). No anti-aliased blur, no semi-transparent pixels, no dithering noise, no upscaled/stretched old sprites.
- Image generation output is a draft: downscale with nearest-neighbour to the target grid and clean up by hand (pixel edit). Record the tool, prompt and edits in the provenance note.
- Must later split into modular layers (GATE 03): keep hair, goggles, scarf, garments and accessories visually distinct; avoid details that only work as one flattened image.
