## Idle (done 2026-11-26)
`python tools/asset-pipeline/animate.py idle` builds a waist-squash breathing loop: pixels above the waist line (top of the belt label) move down 1 px on frames 1 and 2 (frame durations 260/200/260/200 ms). The same transform is applied to every layer, including the `*_full` layers, so any outfit combination breathes identically. Outputs: `assets/characters/trainer/anim_v3/idle/{male,female}/{slot}.webp` (rows = S..SE, columns = frames) and `metadata/trainer_v3_anim_idle.json`. Previews: `metadata/idle_preview_2x.png`, `idle_male.gif`, `idle_female.gif`.

## Owner feedback on the pilot (2026-11-26): "not natural enough"
The owner supplied a commercial farm-sim base sprite sheet as a MOTION reference. It is copyrighted: keep it out of Git (local copy only, `build/motion-ref/`), never trace or copy pixels. Use only these principles, noted by Claude Code:
- Front/back walk: the stepping leg reads as a clearly longer, lower leg with the boot planted; the other leg lifts so its boot is shorter/partly hidden; on passing frames the body rises 1 px and the legs are together.
- Side walk: a real stride. In profile the legs open into a visible V on contact frames (front heel down, back toe pushing), close on passing frames, and the body rises on passing.
- Arms swing opposite to the legs with a clear arc, 2–4 px at the hands, not a 1 px nudge. The reference animates arms as a separate layer per frame, which fits our modular `top`/`gloves` slots.
- Head and hair follow the body bob; nothing else floats.
- Timing: contact frames read longest; avoid equal-height "marching".

## Walk E fix (2026-11-26) — "looks like walking backwards"
Cause: on the stride frames the trailing boot was drawn as a heel strike (toe raised), and f1 and f3 were near-identical, so the near and far legs never swapped. Codex redid e_f1..e_f5: toes always point right; the trailing foot pushes off from the toe with the heel raised; the near leg leads on f1 and the far leg on f4; the planted foot slides left over the cycle; arms swing opposite to the legs. 6/6 TECH-PASS, halo 0, f0 byte-identical. Rule for every side/diagonal walk: boots point in the walking direction, and the back foot is always heel-up/toe-down.
